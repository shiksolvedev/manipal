Frequently Asked Questions

Are Online Manipal degrees recognized and valid?

Yes, all Online Manipal degrees are UGC-entitled and recognized by relevant statutory bodies. They hold the same value as on-campus degrees from Manipal University Jaipur, which is NAAC A+ accredited.

What is the eligibility for online MBA at Manipal?

Candidates must have a Bachelor's degree in any discipline with a minimum of 50% marks from a recognized university. No entrance exam is required for admission to the online MBA program.

How are online exams conducted at Online Manipal?

Exams are conducted online through a proctored system. Students can appear for exams from the comfort of their home using a laptop/desktop with a webcam and stable internet connection.

What kind of placement support does Online Manipal provide?

Online Manipal provides 100% placement assistance including resume building workshops, mock interviews, access to a career portal with 400+ hiring partners, and dedicated career counselors to help you land your dream job.

What is the fee structure for Online Manipal programs?

The fee varies by program. Online MBA typically starts from ₹1,25,000 per year. All programs offer easy EMI options and flexible payment plans to make education affordable for everyone.

Can I pursue an Online Manipal degree while working?

Absolutely! Online Manipal programs are specifically designed for working professionals. The flexible schedule allows you to study at your own pace, access recorded lectures anytime, and balance work-life-study seamlessly.
